Frequently Asked Questions

Which facial is best for my skin type?

+
Our specialists conduct a comprehensive skin analysis during your consultation to recommend the exact facial (Hydra, Gold Radiance, Carbon or Deep Cleansing) matching your skin's moisture, tone and sensitivity.

Is Hydra Facial suitable for all skin types?

+
Yes, Hydra Facial is completely non-invasive and safe for all skin types, including sensitive and acne-prone skin, delivering instant hydration and nourishment.

Does Carbon Laser Treatment have any side effects?

+
Carbon Laser Peel (Hollywood Peel) has zero downtime. There may be mild temporary redness that subsides within an hour, revealing visibly radiant, refined pores.

How many sessions are needed for visible results?

+
You will notice a fresh, radiant glow immediately after the very first session. For lasting correction of hyperpigmentation or acne scars, 3 to 5 sessions are recommended.
